3. Bring to a boil:
Place the covered pot on high heat and bring the water to a rolling boil. 
4. Rest the eggs:
Once the water reaches a rolling boil, immediately remove the pot from the burner. Cover the pot with a lid and let the eggs stand in the hot water. 
For large eggs, let them stand for 9 to 12 minutes. 
Adjust the standing time for different sizes: slightly less time for smaller eggs, and a bit longer for extra-large eggs.
