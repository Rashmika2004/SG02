To boil eggs, place them in a pot, cover with an inch of water, and bring to a rolling boil. Once boiling, remove the pot from the heat, cover it, and let the eggs stand in the hot water for 9-12 minutes for hard-boiled eggs. Immediately transfer the eggs to an ice bath for at least 10 minutes to stop the cooking process and make them easier to peel.
